# Pokédex Content: Legendary Pokémon of the World

Pattern per Pokémon: **01 Pokémon Overview**, **02 Real-World Location** (with "Why here?"), **03 Legend & Design Reference**. Each section ends with its own reference links.

**How to read the labels in section 03**
- *Commonly cited*: fans and Pokémon wikis often name this legend as an inspiration. Game Freak has not confirmed it.
- *Documented in beta development data*: shown in early game files or sprites found by researchers. Stronger evidence than a fan theory, but still not an official statement (used for Celebi).
- *Our interpretation*: a cultural parallel chosen by our team. State this clearly on the site.

**Notes for the site**
- "Title" is our own epithet for each Pokémon. It is not the official Pokédex category.
- Several legends below are living or sacred traditions (Thunderbird, Anangu, Maya). Present them respectfully as inspiration, not as retellings.
- Link status is listed at the end of this file. Click-test every link before submitting.

## ZAPDOS
`slug: zapdos` | Pokédex No. 145

### 01 — Pokémon Overview
| Type | Title | Real-World Location |
|---|---|---|
| Electric / Flying | Thunderstorm Guardian | Lake Maracaibo, Venezuela |

| Height | Weight | In-Game Habitat |
|---|---|---|
| 1.6 m | 52.6 kg | Power Plant (Kanto) |

**Overview**
Zapdos is a Legendary Electric/Flying-type Pokémon associated with thunderstorms and lightning. It is known for its striking yellow feathers and powerful electrical abilities, making it one of the most recognizable members of the Legendary Birds.

References
- [Zapdos (Pokémon), Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Zapdos_(Pok%C3%A9mon))
- [Legendary birds, Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Legendary_birds)

### 02 — Real-World Location
**Lake Maracaibo**
Venezuela

Lake Maracaibo is the largest lake in Venezuela. Where the Catatumbo River flows into it, the Catatumbo lightning storm appears on roughly 140 to 160 nights a year, for up to about nine hours a night. NASA data counts around 250 lightning flashes per square kilometre per year, one of the highest densities measured on Earth. Sailors have used the flashes as a natural lighthouse, known as the Beacon of Maracaibo.

**Why here?**
Zapdos is tied to storms, so we looked for the most reliable storm on the planet. A thunderstorm that returns over the same spot for months of the year feels like the territory of a living creature. Other places also rank high for lightning, so we call this one of the densest, not the single densest.

References
- [Catatumbo lightning, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catatumbo_lightning)
- [Lake Maracaibo, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Maracaibo)

### 03 — Legend & Design Reference
**The Thunderbird** (*Commonly cited*)

The thunderbird is a powerful spirit-being in the traditions of several Indigenous peoples of North America. It is described as a giant bird whose wingbeats make thunder and whose eyes flash lightning. Details differ from nation to nation.

Zapdos shares the storm-bringing bird idea: yellow wings, electricity, and a link to thunderclouds. The legend comes from North America, while the map location was chosen for its lightning, so the two sections look at Zapdos through different lenses.

References
- [Thunderbird (mythology), Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thunderbird_(mythology))

`slug: articuno` | Pokédex No. 144

### 01 — Pokémon Overview
| Type | Title | Real-World Location |
|---|---|---|
| Ice / Flying | Frost Sovereign | Mount Everest, Nepal/China |

| Height | Weight | In-Game Habitat |
|---|---|---|
| 1.7 m | 55.4 kg | Seafoam Islands (Kanto) |

**Overview**
Articuno is a Legendary Ice/Flying-type Pokémon and one of the three Legendary Birds of Kanto. It controls ice and cold air, and snow is said to fall wherever it flies. In the games it hides deep inside the Seafoam Islands.

References
- [Articuno (Pokémon), Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Articuno_(Pok%C3%A9mon))

### 02 — Real-World Location
**Mount Everest**
Nepal and China (Tibet)

Everest is the highest mountain above sea level, about 8,849 m. Its upper slopes are covered by permanent snow and ice, with freezing temperatures and very thin air. The Nepali side lies in Sagarmatha National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

**Why here?**
A bird that freezes the air needs a perch above the clouds. Everest is the highest and one of the coldest places people can reach, remote enough that a legendary bird could stay hidden.

References
- [Mount Everest, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Everest)
- [Sagarmatha National Park, UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/120)

### 03 — Legend & Design Reference
**The Simurgh** (*Our interpretation*)

In Persian mythology the Simurgh is a huge, benevolent bird that lives on a mountain and is said to heal with its feathers. It is often drawn with long, flowing tail plumage.

Articuno's graceful, long tail feathers and its aloof, mountain-dwelling image fit this parallel. No creator has linked Articuno to the Simurgh.

References
- [Simurgh, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simurgh)

## MOLTRES
`slug: moltres` | Pokédex No. 146

### 01 — Pokémon Overview
| Type | Title | Real-World Location |
|---|---|---|
| Fire / Flying | Phoenix of the Ring of Fire | Mount Merapi, Java, Indonesia |

| Height | Weight | In-Game Habitat |
|---|---|---|
| 2.0 m | 60.0 kg | Mt. Ember / Victory Road (Kanto) |

**Overview**
Moltres is a Legendary Fire/Flying-type Pokémon and one of the three Legendary Birds of Kanto. Each flap of its wings throws off flames, and it is said to heal its wounds by bathing in volcanic magma.

References
- [Moltres (Pokémon), Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Moltres_(Pok%C3%A9mon))

### 02 — Real-World Location
**Mount Merapi, Java**
Indonesia

Merapi is one of Indonesia's most active volcanoes, rising north of the city of Yogyakarta. Its name is commonly translated as "Mountain of Fire". Indonesia sits on the Pacific Ring of Fire, a belt of volcanoes and earthquakes around the Pacific Ocean.

**Why here?**
A bird of fire that heals in magma needs a volcano that really erupts. Merapi's repeated eruptions and its fiery name make it a fitting home, and it keeps Moltres far from the other fire legends on the map.

References
- [Mount Merapi, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Merapi)
- [Ring of Fire, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ring_of_Fire)

### 03 — Legend & Design Reference
**The Phoenix** (*Commonly cited*)

The phoenix is a mythical fire bird that burns and is reborn from its own ashes. Similar birds appear in many cultures, such as the Bennu of ancient Egypt.

Moltres' blazing plumage, its fire-and-flight identity, and its magma-healing habit all echo the rebirth theme.

References
- [Phoenix (mythology), Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoenix_(mythology))
- [Bennu, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bennu)

### 02 — Real-World Location
**Seoul**
South Korea

Seoul is the capital of South Korea, a large modern city surrounded by mountains such as Bukhansan. The tiger is a deep-rooted symbol in Korean culture. It appears in folk art and in the Dangun founding myth, and the mascot of the 1988 Seoul Olympics, Hodori, was a tiger.

**Why here?**
Raikou's body looks like a tiger. We moved it from Japan to Korea on purpose, to spread legends across the map and to use Korea's tiger tradition. Its original inspiration in the games is Japanese (see section 03).

### 03 — Legend & Design Reference
**The Raijū** (*Commonly cited*)

The Raijū ("thunder beast") is a creature of Japanese folklore made of lightning. It is said to descend to earth during thunderstorms and may take the shape of an animal such as a cat, dog, or wolf.

Raikou's name, lightning body, and storm-cloud imagery match the Raijū closely. The Korea placement is our own twist.

References
- [Raijū, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raij%C5%AB)

## CELEBI
`slug: celebi` | Pokédex No. 251 (Mythical)

### 01 — Pokémon Overview
| Type | Title | Real-World Location |
|---|---|---|
| Psychic / Grass | Forest Time-Walker | Amazon rainforest, Brazil |

| Height | Weight | In-Game Habitat |
|---|---|---|
| 0.6 m | 5.0 kg | Ilex Forest (Johto, event) |

**Overview**
Celebi is a Mythical Psychic/Grass-type Pokémon that travels through time. It is regarded as a guardian of forests and is said to appear when the forest is at peace.

References
- [Celebi (Pokémon), Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Celebi_(Pok%C3%A9mon))

### 02 — Real-World Location
**Amazon rainforest**
Brazil (and eight other countries)

The Amazon is the largest tropical rainforest on Earth, covering about 5.5 million square kilometres, with roughly 60% inside Brazil. It is home to a very large share of the world's known species.

**Why here?**
Celebi is a forest guardian, and no forest is bigger or more biodiverse. Placing it in the Amazon also reminds visitors that forests need protection.

References
- [Amazon rainforest, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_rainforest)

### 03 — Legend & Design Reference
**Kokopelli** (*Documented in beta development data, not officially confirmed*)

Kokopelli is a flute-playing figure in the art and traditions of Native peoples of the American Southwest, including the Hopi and other Pueblo communities. It is associated with music, fertility, and trickster stories, and its meaning varies between communities.

Early Pokémon Gold and Silver development data (a May 1998 build) contains a black silhouette with three tufts of hair playing a flute. The fan-run archive The Cutting Room Floor identifies it as based on Kokopelli and likely reworked into Celebi. The final design is a cute, fairy-like creature, so only part of the reference is visible today, and how much survives is a matter of interpretation.

The legend comes from the American Southwest, while the map location was chosen for Celebi's role as a forest guardian, so the two sections look at Celebi through different lenses. Present Kokopelli respectfully as a living tradition.

References

- [Regirock (Pokémon), Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Regirock_(Pok%C3%A9mon))

### 02 — Real-World Location
**Giza**
Egypt

The Giza pyramid complex stands at the edge of the desert near Cairo. The Great Pyramid was built more than 4,500 years ago and is the only one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World that still stands.

**Why here?**
Regirock is an ancient stone guardian sealed inside desert ruins. Giza is the most famous example of enormous ancient stonework in a desert setting.

**The Golem** (*Commonly cited*)

In Jewish folklore a golem is a figure shaped from clay or stone and brought to life by sacred words. It serves as a guardian and obeys commands. The best-known tale is set in Prague.

The Regis are widely compared to golems: humanoid bodies of raw material, ancient, and sealed away.

References
- [Golem, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golem)

Kyogre makes oceans bigger, so we gave it the biggest one. Tahiti also sits in the middle of the Pacific, and in Tahitian tradition Taʻaroa is the creator god of the sea, which suits a sea-making Pokémon.

Kyogre's whale-like body and its power over the oceans match the Leviathan image.

### 02 — Real-World Location
**Easter Island (Rapa Nui)**
Chile

**Why here?**
Regigigas is a giant of stone that moved enormous weights. Rapa Nui oral tradition says the moai "walked" to their platforms, which echoes a giant that hauled continents.

**Atlas and the Titans** (*Our interpretation*)

In Greek myth the Titans are an older generation of giants. Atlas, one of them, is condemned to hold up the sky.

Regigigas is a colossal being of immense strength who carried a world-sized load, which parallels Atlas. Like the other Regis, it also belongs to the golem tradition (see Regirock).
